## What to build

- Pick one of 10 listed public service platforms. **Off-list is allowed but scored lower** — if the reviewers have never used it, they can't judge the improvement.
- **Comprehensive proof of concept — rebuild it entirely.** Mock the data, mock the backend, mock the accounts.
- Must open **in a browser**. Mobile apps are not downloaded or reviewed. No exceptions.
- **Citizen side only.** The admin side is assumed to be fine and is not looked at.
- **Ideas over code.** Direct quote of intent: anyone can vibe-code today; what these platforms need is new ideas. Energy goes into interfaces, interactions, and making things easier — not engineering polish.
- Explicit warning against decoration: a site with heavy 3D / Three.js flourish that doesn't help a busy, frustrated citizen scores *worse*, not better.

Our choice: **EPFO / UAN member portal**. Reasoning in [01-problem.md](01-problem.md).

## Team

- Solo or max 2 people.
- **This project is solo** — partner-email field stays blank.
- If there were a partner: both must be registered, and **each must list the other's email**. Reciprocity is the only verification method. One-sided = rejected.

---

## The email rule (the most common disqualifier)

- The email used at registration **is the identity**, for every form, result, and invite.
- A different email, a lost account, or an unregistered partner ID cannot be fixed by the organisers — small team, 5,000+ entries.
- Round 2 must use **the same email as Round 1**, or it reads as a new, unadmitted entrant.

Registered email for this entry: `lokeshkammara@gmail.com`. Do not submit from anything else.

## Submission = 4 things

1. **Live public link** — hosted anywhere (Vercel, ChatGPT sites, whatever). *"If it doesn't open in a browser, it doesn't exist."*
2. **Video, 2:00 maximum** — self-recorded (Loom / OBS / screen capture).
   - Minute 1: using the project as a citizen.
   - Minute 2: how it was built and *why those decisions*. This exists to prove the entrant had real input.
3. **Text summary, exactly 250 words** — what it is and why it's better than the current solution.
4. **Partner's email** — blank for solo.

Plus, implicitly but critically: **working citizen-side login credentials.** If a judge can't get in, the entry is dead.

---

## Hard disqualifiers

- Submitting from a non-registered email
- App-only build with no browser URL
- Missing or broken demo login
- Video over 2:00
- Late, by any margin
- Partner emails not mutually listed (N/A here — solo)

---

## Judging lens

- Graded **as a citizen using it**, not as an engineer reading it.
- Graded **relative to other submissions**, not against an absolute bar.
- Reviewers openly state they will make mistakes at this scale and cannot write per-entry feedback.
- Consequence for us: the entry must be *legible in 60 seconds*. One idea, executed sharply, landing on a problem the reviewer personally recognises.
